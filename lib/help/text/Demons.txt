Demons

Demons are powerful servants of evil and come in many forms. They rely 
on their devilish powers or their brutish strength to survive.
 
The various demonic races include the Emberlords, powerful demons of 
fire; the Servants of The Red Throne, mighty warriors of destruction; 
the Unbound, weaker demons whose ultimate form has three sets of arms, 
but prefers to fight naked; and Iron Demons, whose firepower is 
unsurpassable.
 
Demons cannot eat normal food, but must feast upon the remains of their 
human enemies. They are unaffected by the Eldritch Horror.

Emberlord
Emberlords are furnace-hot demons that wield fire and nether magic. 
Their spells are the most powerful of all demonkind, and at very high 
levels they may even call forth fires directly from hell.

Unbound
Unbound were originally slave demons, but rose up to overthrow their 
masters. They generally take on a humanoid form and are classic demons 
full of malice and cruelty. The ultimate form for this demon is the 
Bladewheel demon, a female demon with three sets of arms and a serpent 
body capable of attacking with six melee weapons!

Iron Demon
Iron Demons are giant humanoid forms, half demon and half machine. They 
are a bit slow and move erratically, but their immense bodies and 
unsurpassable firepower more than make up for this. The walls of the 
dungeon reverberate with their heavy steps!

Iron Demons are one of the very few monster races not to undergo 
evolution, and so almost all their might is available from the 
beginning, although they do gain even more strength later on and their 
offense becomes stronger with experience.
