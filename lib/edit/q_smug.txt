?:[AND [EQU $QUEST73 Taken InProgress] [EQU $WILDERNESS NORMAL] ]
D:Illegal mushrooms are reaching our streets and school. We know the
D:smugglers' base: a small house just north of here. We cannot obtain a
D:warrant, so I am asking for your discreet help. Clear every enemy from
D:the den and report back. You may keep what you find; there is a separate
D:reward for the work.
?:1

?:[AND [EQU $QUEST73 Completed] [EQU $WILDERNESS NORMAL] ]
D:The smugglers are gone. Keep what you recovered, and take this legal
D:mushroom from our evidence stores as your reward. I shall ask no further
D:questions.
?:1

?:[AND [EQU $QUEST73 Failed] [EQU $WILDERNESS NORMAL] ]
D:Then the den remains a problem. I will have to pursue it another way.
?:1

?:[AND [EQU $QUEST73 Taken InProgress] [EQU $WILDERNESS NONE] ]
D:Someone is selling illegal mushrooms in town, and it is not me. The
D:smugglers work from a small house south of the Arena. Clear every enemy
D:from their den and return to me. Keep what you find; I have a separate
D:bonus for your help.
?:1

?:[AND [EQU $QUEST73 Completed] [EQU $WILDERNESS NONE] ]
D:Thank you for clearing the den. Keep your haul, and take this mushroom
D:as my promised bonus.
?:1

?:[AND [EQU $QUEST73 Failed] [EQU $WILDERNESS NONE] ]
D:Too bad. I will have to find another way into their business.
?:1
