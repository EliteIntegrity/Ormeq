?:[EQU $QUEST21 Taken InProgress]
D:A second breach has opened at the edge of town. Five knights
D:went inside; one returned, badly burned and unable to give
D:a clear account. We cannot spare another patrol. Clear every
D:enemy from the site, then report back to me. Take the
D:survivor's condition as warning enough to prepare carefully.
?:1

?:[EQU $QUEST21 Completed]
D:The second breach has been cleared, and the town can breathe
D:again. Our resident wizard has supplied this reward. Take it
D:with the thanks of everyone who lives here.
?:1

?:[EQU $QUEST21 Failed]
D:The second breach remains uncleared. I had hoped to spare
D:more people from it, but this commission has ended. There
D:is no reward to collect.
?:1
