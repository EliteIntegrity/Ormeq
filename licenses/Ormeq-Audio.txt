Production

The sound effects and ambient soundscapes were produced under human direction using Claude
Code-assisted Python synthesis and recorded seeds. PCM samples were
computed from mathematical models; no performers, recordings, third-party
samples, sample libraries or generative-audio service were used for these
effects.

Ormeq - Home Theme (Dark) was created separately with Claude Compose.
AI-assisted composition code placed its notes, melody, harmony and
orchestration; licensed virtual instruments performed the composition in
Ableton Live. Rendered-audio analysis informed revisions under human
direction, review and approval. No generative audio or music model was used.

The theme was supplied as a stereo 24-bit FLAC master. The playable
edition uses a 16-bit PCM WAV delivery copy with
the same duration, dynamics and natural ending. The original master is
retained privately.
