Reserved Font Names
-------------------

Share Tech Mono is released with the Reserved Font Name 'Share'. Under
clause 3 of the OFL, a modified version of it may not be distributed under
that name. Nothing here is modified, so the restriction is not engaged -- it
is recorded because anybody who does modify one of these needs to know.


Bitmap fonts -- lib/xtra/font/*.fon
===================================

Twenty-two Windows bitmap fonts inherited from upstream. **Only the Win32
port reads these**; the SDL3 port opens TrueType faces and never looks in
this directory, and tools/package.ps1 removes it from the SDL3 release
package. They are here because the Win32 build is the known-good baseline
and will not start without at least one of them.

Every line below is the copyright string inside the .fon file itself, read
out of the binary rather than transcribed from anywhere. None has been
modified.

The last two are the ones that matter. They are X11 bitmap fonts that NCD
and Sony contributed to the X distribution, converted to Windows .fon by
somebody upstream, and they carry corporate copyright notices rather than a
grant of the public domain. The X Consortium terms they were released under
permit redistribution provided the notice travels with them; this file is
that notice.

and listed the five TrueType faces we chose, and twenty-two more were
sitting in the tree.
